# Speed level curves

Generated by `cargo run --release -p ofm-core --bin ofm-curves`. Each point is 12 frames of 6 codewords through the channel simulator with a +100 ppm clock offset, 200 ms TXDELAY and a 9 dB peak-to-average limit. "Channel SNR" is the simulator's setting (signal over white noise across 0-24 kHz); "est SNR" is what the receiver reports, which is what rate adaptation compares with each level's threshold. CER is the codeword error rate. Net bps counts delivered payload bytes over the airtime of one frame including TXDELAY, without turnaround.
